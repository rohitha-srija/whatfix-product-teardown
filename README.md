# Whatfix Product Teardown

An independent product teardown exploring how Whatfix could make enterprise software adoption more adaptive and outcome-driven.

## The Question

What happens after you discover where users struggle?

Enterprise software constantly changes. As workflows evolve, user behavior changes, friction appears, and guidance may need to adapt.

## Hypothesis

What if adoption could adapt itself?

I explored a continuous loop:

Detect → Diagnose → Recommend → Test → Measure → Adapt

The goal is not more guidance, but better guidance, proven by outcomes.

## Whatfix Today

I explored Whatfix's existing capabilities across:

- Digital Adoption Platform (DAP)
- Product Analytics
- Mirror
- AI capabilities

The interesting product question is what happens when these capabilities work together to create a more continuous learning loop.

## My Approach

Rather than proposing a large feature first, I focused on validating the hypothesis through a controlled experiment.

Detect → Find friction  
Diagnose → Understand why  
Recommend → Suggest an intervention  
Test → Validate with users  
Measure → Evaluate the outcome  
Adapt → Keep, change, or remove the intervention

Human-approved • AI-assisted

## Example

A hypothetical workflow:

- Identify a significant drop-off at a specific step
- Understand the source of friction
- Recommend contextual guidance
- Test the intervention against a control group
- Measure whether successful task completion improves

Illustrative scenario. Not actual Whatfix data.
